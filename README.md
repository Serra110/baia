# BACK AT IT AGAIN

Back At It Again is a 2D atmospheric horror game focused on psychological horror and storytelling.
You play as Joe, a victim of workplace abuse who finds himself reliving the trauma of his past.
Your goal is to escape, but no matter what you do, you will always find yourself back where you started.

The game features three levels, all representing the same traumatic experience and taking place in the same location. However, each repetition introduces small, almost unnoticeable changes to the environment. Changes that you might perceive, but never fully recognize or remember (lore-wise)
You can try to escape, but you will always end up back at it again.

## Current State

The game currently has a completed MVP, with the core experience and main concept already implemented.
However, many features are still missing or being developed, such as:
- Puzzles and additional gameplay elements
- More horror elements and disturbing encounters
- Jumpscares
- Additional cutscenes
- More lore and story details to better explain Joe's situation
- More configuration options
The main focus for future development is expanding the atmosphere, making the experience more terrifying and giving the player a better understanding of the story.

## How to play

The game is primarily focused on atmosphere, sound and narrative rather than complex gameplay mechanics.
You will explore the environment and experience Joe's attempts to escape his trauma.
There are 3+ levels, each representing another repetition of the same experience, with subtle environmental variations.
More gameplay instructions will be added as the game develops.

## Development

Back At It Again was developed during a 24 hour game jam organized by **Hack Club Haven Lisbon**.
The theme was **Bounce Back**, and we decided to interpret it through the story of someone who repeatedly tries to escape a traumatic experience, only to end up right back where they started.

The game was developed using **Godot**, in 2D.

### Team

- **Martim** — Programming
- **Diogo / Serra110** — Art (mostly UI)
- **David** — Art (mostly character making)

Sound effects used in the game are royalty-free.

AI was used minimally throughout development, mainly to help debug issues and develop some engine-related code. Since we weren't experienced with Godot, writing certain pieces of engine-specific code and dealing with dependencies would have taken too much time, especially considering we only had 24 hours to complete the project.
Despite the time constraints and the unfinished features, we managed to put together a playable MVP that represents our original concept.
